## H1. `TESObjectDOOR::Activate` only queues the transition

**Verdict: confirmed, with clarification.** By the time `Activate` returns (15–22 μs), `queuedTargetLoc.isValid` is still `false`; this was true in all 5 cases. Then:
- `Fader Menu` opens after 0.50–0.65 s (fade);
- about 0.4 s later, `Loading Menu` opens and `kPre` arrives, now with `target_valid=true`;
- 30–38 frames and 0.91–1.06 s elapse from `Activate` to `kPre`.

`Activate` is called **off the main thread**: all 44 calls came from 6 different worker threads. 39 of 44 are NPCs passing through load doors in unloaded cells throughout Skyrim.

```
  -1052.4 ms  f977    -  27480  hook.TESObjectDOOR.Activate.enter  {"doorCell": "WhiterunPlainsDistrict04 [0001A276]", "destCell": "WhiterunBanneredMare [0001605E]"}
  -1052.4 ms  f977    -  27480  hook.TESObjectDOOR.Activate.leave  {"result": true, "queuedValid": false, "dur_us": 22}
   -407.8 ms  f1015   -  34784  event.Menu  {"menu": "Fader Menu", "opening": true}
     +0.0 ms  f1015   M  27868  event.Menu  {"menu": "Loading Menu", "opening": true}
     +0.2 ms  f1015   M  27868  event.PositionPlayer  {"name": "kPre", ..., "target_valid": true, "target_interior": "WhiterunBanneredMare [0001605E]"}
```

**Open question:** who populates `queuedTargetLoc`, and when. This probably happens when the fade completes. The player's `MoveTo_Impl` is called neither for a door nor for `coc` (0.3.0), so this is a different path.

## H2. Where `SetCurrentCell` fits among the `PositionPlayerEvent` phases

**Verdict: clarified.** Neither `kPreUpdatePackages` nor `kPostUpdatePackages` occurs in any of the 17 transitions; the order is always `kPre → kPost → kFinish`.

`TES::SetCurrentCell` (0.3.0 data):
- is called **only when entering an interior** — both via `coc` and through a door, in both cases from `AE#40744+0x153`;
- is not called at all when exiting to an exterior: a different path is used there, `GridCellArray.DetachAll` and `SetCenter`;
- the call occurs immediately after `kPre`/`ActorCell leave` and takes almost the entire interval until `ActorCell enter` (600.4 of 601.2 ms and 449.7 of 449.9 ms);
- for `coc`, it is called by `PlayerCharacter::CenterOnCell_Impl` (`AE#22873+0x60`, console command).

## H3. Where and how loading occurs

**Verdict: confirmed, with clarification.** There are three different paths.

**1. Interior under the loading screen** (door or `coc`):
- loading is synchronous, on the main thread, between `ActorCell leave` and `enter`;
- a cold load takes 383–642 ms;
- there are no `CellLoaderTask` instances;
- the entire transition fits within **one frame**: the frame counter does not change from the opening to the closing of `Loading Menu`.

**2. Exterior under the loading screen** (world change, `coc` to an exterior, loading a save):
- also without `CellLoaderTask`;
- the 25 grid cells load synchronously in 2.2–2.6 s;
- at the end, 26 `CellFullyLoaded` events arrive within 2 ms from worker threads.

**3. Exterior while walking** — asynchronous:
- `CellLoaderTask::Run`, immediately followed by `Finish`, executes on **one** IO thread (9068), taking 0.9–17.8 ms each;
- `PostProcess` executes on 6 worker threads 2–20 ms after `Run`;
- the order is always `Run → Finish → PostProcess`, 40 out of 40;
- the task's `cell` field is null: the cell is loaded from the world's `x/y` coordinates.

```
| Run t, s | world    | cell     | xy      | Run tid | Run, ms | Finish tid | PostProcess tid | Run→PostProcess, ms |
| 147.840  | 0000003C | 00000000 | [2, -5] | 9068    | 17.6    | 9068       | 1244            | 19.7 |
| 147.858  | 0000003C | 00000000 | [3, -5] | 9068    | 1.9     | 9068       | 27480           | 17.4 |
```

## H4. The cell buffer speeds up re-entry

**Verdict: partial.**

| Case | First entry: kPre→enter / Loading Menu | Re-entry | CellFullyLoaded on re-entry |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interior through a door (Bannered Mare) | 418.6 / 693.9 ms | **16.2 / 291.5 ms** | no: the cell remained loaded |
| Exterior through a door (WhiterunWorld) | — (loaded from save) | 26.6 / 215.1 ms | no |
| Exterior via `coc` (Riverwood) | 2618.4 / 2784.6 ms | **35.4 / 185.5 ms** | no |
| Interior via `coc` (Bannered Mare) | 382.6 / 743.7 ms | 641.6 / 975.4 ms; after `pcb` 466.6 / 806.3 ms | **yes, every time** |

With `coc`, the interior reloads every time, even without `pcb`, despite `uInterior Cell Buffer=3`.

**0.3.0:** after exiting through a door to Riverwood and re-entering through a door, the tavern entered via `coc` also reloaded: `SetCurrentCell` took 449.7 ms, and `CellFullyLoaded` arrived again.

All reload cases have one thing in common: between the two visits, the exterior grid was fully loaded under a loading screen (world change or `coc` to an exterior). On fast re-entry into Bannered Mare, this did not happen: the exit led to the same WhiterunWorld, whose grid remained loaded.

**Confirmed in 0.5.0** — state changes of the retained Bannered Mare:

```
 76.864 ActorCell.enter          kAttached   → kUnattached   exited through door: cell in buffer
 93.160 GridCellArray.DetachAll  kUnattached → kUnloaded     coc Riverwood: world change WhiterunWorld → Tamriel
112.361 LoadingMenu.open         kUnloaded   → kDataLoaded   coc WhiterunBanneredMare: loading again (441 ms)
123.241 ActorCell.enter          kAttached   → kUnattached   coc Riverwood (same world)
123.483 tick                     kUnattached → kUnloaded     30 ms after CellLoaderTask interior=true for 000133C8
```

There are two causes of unloading:
1. **World change.** The interior is unloaded while the old world's grid is being dismantled (`DetachAll`). Entry checks in `DetachAll` in 0.5.1 will show the exact call.
2. **Eviction.** The interior buffer has 3 slots (H9), and preloaded interiors behind doors occupy the same slots. In Riverwood, the game preloaded 3 interiors and evicted Bannered Mare.

Therefore, `coc` to an interior that was open before the world change always loads it again.

**The exterior remains loaded while the player is in an interior** (0.3.0 snapshots): in the interior, all 25 grid cells are in `kUnattached`, and `grid` = [-1,-1]. Therefore, exiting back takes ~30 ms.

### Why the loading screen lasts longer than the cell load

Example — entering Bannered Mare (0.4.0):

| Phase | Time |
|---|---|
| screen opens → `kPre` | 0 ms |
| `kPre` → `enter` (cell load) | 450 ms |
| `enter` → `kPost` | 6 ms |
| **`kPost` → `kFinish`** | **271 ms** |
| `kFinish` → screen closes | 1 ms |

The `kPost → kFinish` tail is 75–356 ms across all 17 transitions. It barely depends on whether the cell was loaded (Bannered Mare: 270 ms both cold and from the buffer). Therefore, even with a fast buffer load (16–35 ms), the loading screen still remains for 170–290 ms.

No events occur in this window, and the frame counter does not increase: the main thread is busy with something synchronous.

**What 0.5.0 showed:**
- **The tail is not waiting for objects to load.** At `kPost`, the cell already has `refsFullyLoaded=true` and `queuedRefs=0` in all 7 transitions.
- **This is computation, not waiting.** According to the profiler (~1000 samples per second), 97% of samples in the `kPost → kFinish` window are in `SkyrimSE.exe` code rather than system wait functions.
- **The work is spread across many functions.** None accounts for more than 11% of the window: `AE#64742`, `AE#65030`, `AE#62405`, `AE#62314`, `AE#67123`, `AE#64787`, `AE#65019`, `AE#67122`.
- **This is Havok physics simulation.** `tools/name_functions.py` identified the functions from `SkyrimSE.exe`'s own metadata: RTTI virtual-function tables, strings in the code, and direct callers.

| Function | Share of samples in window | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `AE#62314` | 59 | references the strings `MiNumJacobians`, `MiNumEntities` — constraint solver, Jacobian assembly |
| `AE#62405` | 99 | recursive; called from `hkpMoppBvTreeShape::vfunc[0xb..0xd]` — queries to collision MOPP trees |
| `AE#64787` | 66 | called from `hkpBreakableConstraintData`/`hkpMalleableConstraintData::vfunc[0xf]` — constraint construction |
| `AE#80438` | 9 | string `TtNarrowPhase`; called from `hkpContinuousSimulation::vfunc[0x11]` (string `TtCollide`) — narrow collision phase |
| `AE#65030`, `AE#65029`, `AE#65019` | 146 / 16 / 13 | recursive traversal, called near `hkpPredGskfAgent` — convex-body collision agents (GSK) |

- **Conclusion:** between `kPost` and `kFinish`, the game synchronously runs physics-world steps for the new location — collisions (broad and narrow phases, MOPP, GSK) and the constraint solver. The tail is longer where there are more physics bodies: Bannered Mare 270–286 ms, Sleeping Giant 227 ms, Riverwood street 133–139 ms.

**What starts the steps** (0.6.0, full stack unwinding; the chain is the same in all 5 transitions, 97–99% of samples in the window):

```
AE#40438 (transition function)
 > AE#41051                         PositionPlayerEvent dispatch
 > AE#13419 = TES[+0x68]::vfunc[1]  TES as BSTEventSink<PositionPlayerEvent>
 > AE#13676 > AE#77921              TES code
 > AE#77851 = bhkWorld::vfunc[0x32] Bethesda world step; step is a constant from a global (movss xmm1,[rip+…])
 > AE#61410                         hkpWorld::stepDeltaTime(float): this->simulation (+0x10) → vfunc[3]
 > hkpSimulation::vfunc[3]          ~67% collide ("TtCollide" → "TtNarrowPhase" → "LtProcessCollColl", "LtBvTree3")
                                    ~29% integrate ("TtPhysics" → "LtIntegrate" → "MiNumJacobians" solver)
```

- According to the machine code, `AE#13419` handles event types 0 (`kPre`) and 3 (`kPost`). Physics warm-up is `TES`'s response to `kPost`.
- The settings dump contains **`fWarmStartMaxTime:HAVOK = 4`** and **`bAllowDeactivationWhileWarmStarting:HAVOK = 0`**.
- **Hypothesis:** this is Havok "warm starting" — the world is stepped until bodies settle, but for no more than 4 seconds of simulated time.

**Test in 0.7.0 — hypothesis confirmed.** A detour of `hkpWorld::stepDeltaTime` (`AE#61410`) counts steps, and a detour of `AE#13419` times the `TES` handler. The last two transitions were made after switching `fWarmStartMaxTime` to 0 in memory:

| Transition | `fWarmStartMaxTime` | `kPost→kFinish` | physics steps | simulated | `TES` on `kPost` | loading screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| enter Bannered Mare (first) | 4 | 290.5 ms | 241 | 4.02 s | 284.8 ms | 316 ms |
| exit to street | 4 | 179.5 ms | 241 | 4.02 s | 179.3 ms | 216 ms |
| enter Bannered Mare | **0** | **1.6 ms** | 1 | 0.02 s | 1.4 ms | **26 ms** |
| exit to street | **0** | 60.8 ms | 1 | 0.02 s | 6.1 ms | 106 ms |

- The step is fixed at 1/60 s: 241 steps = 4.02 s = `fWarmStartMaxTime` plus one step. Bodies do not "settle" early: warm-up always runs to the limit.
- `TES` warms up only the world the player entered: steps are counted in a single `hkpWorld` (see H11–H12).
- At 0, the user noticed neither falling objects nor strange NPCs. But these were repeat entries after the cells' physics had already "settled". Entry into a cell whose physics has never been stepped is the 0.7.1 test.
- **Residual at 0 when exiting outside.** 55 ms elapse after `TES` finishes `kPost` but before `kFinish`. This is another `kPost` subscriber or code between dispatches. At 4, this residual is absent (`TES` leave and `kFinish` occur in the same 0.1 ms). Something appears to wait for background work that had time to finish during warm-up. The profiler is enabled in 0.7.1 to determine this.
- **0.7.1 answer: waiting for the IO queue.**
  - In the `kPost → kFinish` window on exits from Bannered Mare (55–60 ms), 80–90% of samples belong to one chain: `AE#40438` (transition function) directly calls `AE#75708`, which waits in `KERNELBASE`.
  - `AE#75708` is also called by `SetCurrentCell` (`AE#13316`), exterior loading (`AE#13428`), and `AE#40744`; through `AE#75709 → AE#75643`, it is connected to the same code as `IOManager::vfunc[0x14]` (`AE#75710`) and `BSTaskManager::vfunc[0x14]`. It appears to mean "wait for the background loading-task queue".
  - With warm-up at 4, the queue empties during the physics steps, so no wait is visible. Exiting Belethor's shop to the adjacent street waited for nothing (5.6 ms).
  - Which tasks are in the queue has not yet been determined. `CellLoaderTask` hooks did not fire in this window.

**Profiler overhead.** With full stack unwinding, the tail in 0.6.0 grew by 15–22%: Bannered Mare 286 → 350 ms, Whiterun street 177–181 → 212 ms. Absolute durations are inflated while the profiler is enabled; the tree structure does not change.

**A cold load, by contrast, consists mostly of waiting.**
- Within `SetCurrentCell` and exterior loading, 70% of samples are in `ntdll.dll` in the chain `AE#75686 < AE#75708 < AE#13316/13317`: the main thread waits for background resource-loading tasks.
- `AE#75676 < AE#75696` processes them on the main thread.
- These IDs are located near `BSModelDB` (75782).

The autotest scenario has been fixed: door and `coc` are now tested separately, and `purge` runs outside the cell under test (`docs/ORIGINAL_TASK.md`, 5.3).

## H5. `currentQueuedX/Y` is preloading

**Verdict: confirmed** (from 0.3.0 data; in 0.2.0 I mistakenly considered the hypothesis disproved).
- Upon entering a new exterior cell, `QueueCellLoad` is called for the row missing from a grid centered on that cell. The callers are `AE#13288+0x468` and `+0x4c8`; this is the same function that later calls `SetCenter`.
- Immediately afterward, `currentQueuedX/Y` shifts to the new cell, while `currentGridX/Y` remains unchanged:

```
| t, s    | QueueCellLoad xy | grid      | queued    | player cell |
| 130.603 | [3..7, -14]      | [5, -11]  | [5, -11]  | [5, -12] |
| 133.483 | [2, -14..-10]    | [5, -11]  | [5, -12]  | [4, -12] |   ← queued leads grid
```

- By the time of `GridCellArray::SetCenter`, the grid catches up: `grid == queued` (5 out of 5 cases in 0.2.0).
- 5 `CellLoaderTask` instances are queued 0.2–0.4 ms after `ActorCell enter` (in one case, after 22.8 ms).
- The grid shift itself (`SetCenter`, 0.2–1.2 ms on the main thread) occurs only after **4.9–7.1 s**. If the player goes through a door during this time, no shift occurs at all.
- After the shift, these 5 cells attach (`CellFullyLoaded` from worker threads) within 35–280 ms.

```
batch t=147.840 xy=[2..6, -5]   prev ActorCell enter [4,-3] 0.2 ms before   next SetCenter [4,-3] after 5397 ms
batch t=159.170 xy=[2..6, -6]   prev ActorCell enter [4,-4] 0.2 ms before   next SetCenter [4,-4] after 7147 ms
```

**Open questions:**
- what holds the shift for 5–7 s. It may be `gridBufferDistanceX/Y`: in 0.3.0 I will log the player position at `SetCenter`;
- `TES::GetAERuntimeData()` returned `nullptr` (the pointer at `TES+0x120` is null), so `gridBufferDistance` cannot be read through the NG accessor.

## H6. Event order

**Verdict: confirmed** (12 out of 12 transitions). The order is:
1. `Loading Menu` open;
2. `kPre`;
3. `ActorCell leave`;
4. loading;
5. `ActorCell enter`;
6. `ActorLocationChange`;
7. `CellFullyLoaded` — only if the cell was not already loaded;
8. `kPost`;
9. `kFinish`;
10. `Loading Menu` close.

Diagram 3.6 in docs/ENGINE_MAP.md, which places the events after `kFinish`, is incorrect.

## H7. `CellLoaderTask` with `interior=true`

**Verdict: confirmed** (0.5.0). With the default `bPreloadLinkedInteriors=0`, there are no such tasks (0.2.0: 0 out of 40). The plugin enables the setting in game memory after data load (`Skyrim.ini` is not modified), and the game itself begins loading interiors behind doors near the player.

How this appears in the 0.5.0 trace:
- 15 of 35 `CellLoaderTask` tasks have `interior=true`, `world=null`, and `cell` = interior FormID;
- `Run` and `Finish` execute on the same IO thread as the exterior, taking 1.8–7.5 ms each;
- an interior is queued when the player enters an exterior cell near a door: Bannered Mare is queued together with row `x=8` upon entering [6,-2].

Result for the retained interior:

```
55.580 tick                kUnloaded → kDataLoaded → kUnattached   (CellLoaderTask interior=true, 0001605E)
61.463 TES.SetCurrentCell  kUnattached → kAttached                 SetCurrentCell 19.7 ms (cold — 420–600 ms)
```

Sleeping Giant, which had not yet been entered in this run, was also preloaded: entry through the door took 34.8 ms.

**Conclusion for "instant entry":** for entry through a door, the built-in setting handles the "cell load" portion. The `kPost → kFinish` tail remains (see H4).

**Interiors behind doors while the player is outside (H11):**
- They are not rendered: absent from the scene in all 435 samples.
- NPCs in ordinary interiors are middle-low without 3D. Nevertheless, they sometimes move (coarse schedule-driven AI): Dragonsreach basement — 22 of 42, Dragonsreach — 17 of 113, Jorrvaskr — 4 of 115.
- NPCs in interiors that the game **preloaded** (`bPreloadLinkedInteriors`) are **middle-high with loaded 3D**: Bannered Mare 70 of 94, Riverwood Trader 20 of 24, Whiterun shops, Sleeping Giant.

**Exterior while the player is inside (H12):**
- The grid is not rendered.
- NPCs outside do **not** drop to low: all are middle-high with 3D, and roughly one in five moves within 5 s. Exterior AI continues to run.

**Physics:** not measured in 0.6.0. Reading `hkpSimulation` time using a guessed layout failed validation. In 0.7.0, steps are counted directly in `hkpWorld::stepDeltaTime` for each physics world.

## H14–H16. Interior "as an adjacent cell" (static analysis before 0.8.0)

User request: while the player is in Whiterun's square, the Bannered Mare interior must be loaded just like adjacent exterior cells: NPC AI fully active, preferably with rendering possible. Findings in `SkyrimSE.exe` code (`dumpbin /disasm`, `tools/name_functions.py --xref`):

**Loading and retention:**
- **`AE#18540` = `ExteriorCellLoader::QueueInteriorCellLoad(loader, cell, bool)`** — one of three functions that create `CellLoaderTask` (`AE#18526`, `AE#18540`, and `AE#18541` = `QueueCellLoad` take the `CellLoaderTask` vtable address). What it does:
  - writes `cell` (`+0x28`), `interior = 1` (`+0x3C`), and a flag (`+0x3D`) to the task;
  - places the task in `queuedCellMap` and passes it to `IOManager`;
  - if the cell is already in `TES::interiorBuffer` (`0x1401A61A0`), loads nothing and calls `0x1401A61D0`.
- **`0x1401A61D0`** — LRU insertion into the interior buffer:
  - a cell already in the buffer moves to the front;
  - a new cell evicts the last one, which is unloaded (`0x1401B66D0`).
- **`AE#13378`** — wrapper for `QueueInteriorCellLoad(singleton, cell, true)`. It is called by `AE#40845` from `PlayerCharacter::Update` (vfunc `0xAD`): this is how `bPreloadLinkedInteriors` works.
- **Conclusion:** calling `AE#18540` for the desired interior once every couple of seconds will keep it loaded and prevent eviction. In 0.8.0: `[Preload] bKeepInterior`.

**Physics:**
- **`bhkWorld::vfunc[0x32]` (`AE#77851`, shared by `bhkWorld`/`bhkWorldM`)** — one world step.
  - Signature `(bhkWorld*, int)`; the warm-up loop passes 1.
  - Step length comes from globals `0x14202C688`/`C690`. The warm-up loop places `fMaxTime:HAVOK` there.
  - If byte `bhkWorld+0xC5F9` is 0, the function returns immediately. NG names this field `toggleCollision`.
- **`AE#77921`** — warm-up loop: `vfunc[0x32](world, 1)` until the sum of steps exceeds `fWarmStartMaxTime`. It is called by `AE#13676` (warm-up preparation) from `TES`.
- **0.8.0 plan:** detour `AE#77851`. Immediately after the player world's step, the same call steps the retained interior's world; byte `+0xC5F9` is set to 1 for the duration of the step.

**AI:** `Actor` has `MoveToHigh()` (vfunc `0xF1`). Try raising interior NPCs to high once per second and see whether they remain there.

**Rendering:**
- `cell3D` of an interior detached from the scene (`kUnattached`) is a ready-made node tree without a parent.
- 0.8.0 plan: attach it to the exterior scene under a custom `NiNode` with transform `T_exterior_door · Rz(180°) · T_interior_door⁻¹`. Then the interior door will coincide with the exterior door; there is also a variant raised above the building.
- Transform updates use the `NiUpdateData::kDisableCollision` flag so that the interior's Havok bodies are not moved.
- The node is detached before any loading screen and when the cell state changes.

**How the game decides whether to render an object** (1.6.1170 machine code):
- **`NiAVObject::Cull` (`0x140D1C570`).** If `AppCulled` (flag `0x1`) is set, the object is skipped. Otherwise, `NiCullingProcess::vfunc[0x16]` is called.
- **`BSCullingProcess::Process` (`AE#76553`):**
  - an object with zero `worldBound` radius is skipped unless `kAlwaysDraw` (`1 << 11`) is set;
  - no checks occur in `kAllPass`/`kAllFail` modes;
  - otherwise, the view-frustum check (`vfunc[0x1C]` with `worldBound`) and occlusion check (`0x140E320B0`) run;
  - then the object's `OnVisible` (`vfunc[0x34]`) runs.
- **`NiNode::OnVisible` (`AE#70307`)** culls children with the same `Cull`.
- **`BSFadeNode::OnVisible` (`AE#105485`).** Every frame, it recalculates `currentFade` (`+0x130`) from screen size (`0x14147A160`). It culls children only if `currentFade > 0` and `NiAVObject::fadeAmount` (`+0x100`) ≠ 0.
- **`BSTriShape::OnVisible` (`AE#70926`)**, if shader data is present (`+0x128`), adds itself to render passes (`process->vfunc[0x18]`).
- **0.8.4: hook `OnVisible` on seven classes.** These are `NiNode`, `BSFadeNode`, `BSMultiBoundNode`, `BSMultiBoundRoom`, `BSTriShape`, `BSDynamicTriShape`, and `BSSubIndexTriShape`. The hook counts calls for attached nodes, door copies, and the tavern building (which the game renders itself).

**How the engine renders a scene with a second camera** (`BSCubeMapCamera::RenderCubemap` `AE#107562` → `0x1414ED920`, 3D menu `AE#52727`, `Main::RenderWorld` `AE#107142`):
1. `RenderTargetManager::SetCurrentRenderTarget(slot, target, mode, updateViewport)` (`AE#77453`) and `SetCurrentDepthStencilTarget` (`AE#77454`). 2. `accumulator->StartAccumulating(camera)` (vfunc `0x25`). The accumulator (`BSShaderAccumulator::Create(0x63)`) receives `activeShadowSceneNode` (`SSN[0]`, `SSN[1]` for the 3D menu), `renderMode`, and `eyePosition`.
3. `BSCullingProcess::CullingContext` (accumulator, camera, `&camera->viewFrustum`, scene, `cullMode`) → `BSCullingProcess::Process` (`AE#106921`). The culling process is shared across the engine: there is no need to create a custom one.
4. `Renderer::SubmitAccumulator(camera, accumulator, flags)` (`AE#106436`): `State::SetCameraData(camera)`, `SetCurrentAccumulator`, `StartAccumulating`, `FinishAccumulatingDispatch`, `FinishAccumulatingPostResolveDepth`.

The engine caches camera data separately for each `NiCamera` (`State::cameraDataCacheA`, `FindCameraDataCache`). Therefore, our camera clone does not touch the main camera's "previous" matrices needed for TAA. `Main::RenderWorld` sets the world camera itself (`Renderer::StartAccumulating`), so a pass before it does not corrupt the main frame.

**What the game does with attached cells every frame.** Both traversals use **either** the player’s interior **or** the exterior grid and the world’s persistent cell:
- `AE#13291(TES, dt)` (task `AE#39067`) adds `dt` to the cell time `AE#400333`, then:
  - interior — `AE#18889`: step its Havok world (vfunc `0x32`), selectively update the fifth child of `cell3D` using this time, update flickering lights (`loadedData + 0x58`), and run tasks `AE#18891`;
  - grid — `AE#15963` → `AE#18890` for each cell: update references from `loadedData + 0x40` (`AE#18894`, using time `AE#400333`).
- `AE#13325(TES, jobs)` (task `AE#39045`) updates animation graphs for the same references (`AE#18899` in tasks or `AE#18900` immediately). `AE#18900` skips a cell that is not in `kAttached`.

**Conclusion.** The game cannot be fooled by changing only "where the player is": there is no single such location. Cell adjacency consists of three things:
- the `kAttached` state (used, for example, to select animation tasks in `AE#41466`);
- the traversals for "player interior or grid" (`AE#13291`, `AE#13325`);
- shared coordinates.

The first two can be reproduced by calling the game’s own functions. The interior has its own coordinates and its own Havok world, so distances to the player do not match. The camera distance (`AE#37277`) has already been overridden to enable full processing; the image is provided by the portal camera.

- **The player has a single “identity”:** pointer `AE#403521` (2,843 references in 1,349 functions), handle `AE#403520` (112 references), FormID 0x14. Papyrus `Game.GetPlayer` (`AE#55469`) simply reads the pointer. Code with the loaded pointer: passes it as an argument 1,761 times, compares it 424 times, reads fields only on `PlayerCharacter` 323 times, and writes 113 times. The engine will not recognize a second `PlayerCharacter`, an actor double, or a shadow copy as the player.
- **There is no dedicated thread on the far side.** Interior Havok steps run in `BSJobs` tasks, each time on a different worker thread (five different ones in 0.8.32); actor updates and cell traversal happen there too, interleaved with the street. The hypothesis “replace the pointer for the cell thread behind the portal” is wrong in both respects: there is no such thread, and therefore nothing to replace it with.
- **What can be salvaged:** a `thread_local` frame for the duration of far-side work (like `Portal::ActorScope` for the world camera), and replacing not the player but the player’s location. There are 90 locations in 78 functions (`AE#18889`, `AE#36661`, `AE#39322`, `AE#37618`, `AE#39673`, etc.) that take only the player’s position, angle, or cell. All 2,843 pointer reads are 7-byte instructions: they can be rewritten as stubs to intercept what the far side asks the player for.
- **Verdict:** the foundation is location substitution. Virtual player T22 only partially addresses this; it does not cover T21, T23, T27, or the `PortalActors` hooks (camera, `kNotVisible`, LOD, fading).

### T30 investigation without a run: tags for reactions to location changes

Details: `docs/tasks/results/T30.md`, `docs/LOCATION_TRIGGERS.md`, sections 4.5–4.9 and 12. Based on `Skyrim.esm`, `Update.esm`, DLC, and NG headers; not tested in-game.
- **Merchant and homeowner greetings on entry are triggered by the `CLOC` quest `WIGreeting`** (aliases `Merchant` and `HomeOwner`, scene `WIGreetingScene` with `BeginOnQuestStart`, no `RunOnce`: it runs on every entry). The innkeeper works the same way, via `WITavern`. Merchant `Hello` dialogue (6,687 `INFO` records in `Dialogue<город>`) is a separate layer without `CLOC`; the Story Manager filter does not affect it.
- **Nine tags** (`story`, `once`, `scene`, `greets`, `encounter`, `technical`, `restarts`, `placeKind`, `placeSpecific`) are derived from quest data, nodes, scenes, aliases, and dialogue topics. A quest’s EditorID is stored in memory (`TESQuest::formEditorID`); EditorID exceptions also work for third-party mods.
- **From the user’s perspective or “as in vanilla,” R8 was wrong for 16 of 17 quests:** it suppressed greetings, radiant encounters, the courier, and the `FreeformWinterholdCollegeA` errand.
- **T30 policy** (accepted by the user on 05.11.2026): suppress `restarts & !story & !greets & !once`, and only if the quest being restarted is already running in the destination cell. In vanilla, this is one `BardSongStarter` while a song is already playing. Without the “already running” condition, in a world without loading screens a silent bard will not start singing: only the starter starts the song. The direct `restarts` indicator (a `StopAllSongs` call) exists only in script bytecode, so the tag is indirect.

### T38, T39, T41 investigations without a run: player and cell-loading maps, multiplayer

08.11.2026, Ghidra database (T33–T36). Details: `docs/PLAYER_MAP.md`, `docs/CELL_LOADING_MAP.md`, `docs/MULTIPLAYER_MODS.md`, `docs/tasks/results/T38.md`, `T39.md`, `T41.md`; bulk reads: `docs/tasks/results/T38-T39-batches.md`. “Verified” means the coordinator checked against `asm`/`xrefs`. - **Frame order (main thread, verified):** `Main::Update` (`AE#36564`) `+0x6E` → `AE#40438` (player transition to `queuedTargetLoc`, `player+0x648…+0x68A`; inside it, `+0x839` is the Story Manager queue `AE#32484`) → `+0x2A7` → `AE#36581` → `+0xAD` player vfunc `0xAD` (`PlayerCharacter::Update`). Player animation and movement use their own `BSJobs` tasks (`AE#39055`, `AE#41445`).
- **All player transitions except walking go through `AE#40438`.** The queue is populated by `AE#40442`: door (`AE#17924+0x1B1` from `HandleOpen` `AE#17926` or fade callback `AE#17968`), `MoveTo_Impl`, fast travel; `coc` calls `AE#40438` directly. `TESObjectDOOR::Activate` does not enqueue it (H1). Phases in `AE#40438` (verified): screen `AE#13363` `+0x4FB` → `kPre` `+0x55A` → `AE#40744` `+0x6D4` → `kPost` `+0x885` → `AE#75708(5)` `+0x8B2` → `kFinish` `+0x8DD` → close `+0x952`; matches the 0.7.1 trace (`seq 713–732`, in-game).

It also changes climate, grass, map markers, camera collision (`AE#50833` `+0x271` — the only call, verified), and the water-height global `AE#379980` (listener `AE#32154`). A portal that does not call `SetParentCell` will leave all of this stale.
- **`SetCurrentCell`:** the fourth argument is not read (H8); entering an interior disables the grid without unloading it (`AE#19130(cell,0)`), disables the exterior Havok world, and resets the portal graph; buffer unloading inside it occurs only with `bPreemptivelyUnloadCells`. Buffer lengths come from the INI settings `uInterior/uExterior Cell Buffer` (H9, based on code).
- **The `TES` buffer is modified off the main thread too.** `CellLoaderTask::PostProcess` (`AE#18529`) calls `AE#13434` (buffer, without a lock) → unload `AE#13721`. `PostProcess` calls `AE#75705`, which is called by `Main::Update+0x5C7` and `AE#75708` (main thread), and by `AE#36565` ← `Job_IO` (`AE#39084`, jobs; verified). In traces, `PostProcess` ran on 5–6 job threads (in-game). `Main::Update` launches stage 1–4 jobs at `+0x82F…+0x859` (after the player and grid shift), but waits only for stage 1 at `+0x8B3` (verified); the stage in which `Job_IO` runs was not found in code. Therefore, **hypothesis:** `PostProcess` runs concurrently with the main thread. T40 will verify this; until then, our `AE#13721` detour must not assume it is on the main thread.
- **One instance everywhere:** `interiorCell`, one grid for one world, exterior Havok world `AE#400440`, portal graph, and `ExteriorCellLoader` queue key are all `(x,y)` without a world. The game’s structures cannot accommodate grids from two worlds (gates, 5.14 section 2). Quest aliases are searched in `TES` cells (`AE#25063`).
- **Camera (5.14, section 4):** `PlayerCamera` gets the target by handle (`+0x3C`), the cell and `bhkWorld` from the target (`+0x60`); the pose comes from states via singletons. The portal camera needs its own pose; camera collision is not needed for rendering.
- **Player access paths:** pointer `AE#403521`, second pointer `AE#401069` (549 references; written only by constructor `AE#40411+0xFC7` and destructor `AE#40412+0xE3`; verified — equals the first during gameplay and is used for identity comparisons), handle `AE#403520` (incorrectly named `gBarterMenu_TargetRefHandle` in the database), FormID 0x14, virtual `GetIsPlayerOwner` (`AE#40691`, always 1).

Our plugin overrides `AE#19784` only for subtitles. Task T22/T32; direct reads of the AI player’s `data.location`/`parentCell` are also covered there (`results/T38-T39-batches.md`, batch 3).
- **Multiplayer (T41):** Skyrim Together Reborn and SkyMP represent another player as an ordinary NPC; neither has a second `PlayerCharacter` (confirming T31); nobody keeps cells beyond the vanilla grid; nobody has solved sound through a door. TE suppresses detection in `AE#42704` (the only call is `DoDetectionJob+0x6D2`, verified) — a point for T22/T32.

Disabling the grid on entry (`AE#19130(cell,0)`) leaves its collision in the Havok world.

### T42, T43, T44 investigations without a run: single-instance state, NPC passage through doors, render-view map

09.11.2026. Documents: `docs/SINGLE_INSTANCE.md`, `docs/NPC_DOORS.md`, `docs/RENDER_VIEW_MAP.md`; results and batches: `docs/tasks/results/T42.md`, `T43.md`, `T44.md`, `T42-T44-batches.md`. “Verified” notes mean the coordinator checked against the database.

- **There is one exterior Havok world per game**, not per worldspace: `GetbhkWorld` (`AE#18995+0xF`) returns `*AE#400440` for any exterior cell; it is written only by `AE#19193+0x111` and `AE#19194+0xB2` (verified). For gates, bodies from two worlds will end up in one Havok world.
- **Gates are an engine limitation:** one grid, one `worldSpace` (47 direct readers and 47 through `AE#13314`), and the `ExteriorCellLoader` and world-cell table key is `(x,y)` without a world; there is one Havok world, and the sky cell is updated only for the current world. Batch B: 11 functions look up a cell or reference by `(x,y)` in the player’s world and will break for the gate world; this can be fixed by using the world of the cell or reference itself (`AE#18236`, `AE#19826`, `AE#32057` verified). The world beyond the gate needs its own cell list outside `TES`.
- **A loaded but non-current interior is invisible to “current location” helpers:** `TES::Pick` (`AE#13371`, actor and player LOS, also called from jobs; verified), `TES::GetCell(pos)` (`AE#13322`), `AE#13306`, and `Pathing` slots 2–6. Batch A: all 31 readers of `interiorCell` make a binary “inside/outside” decision. Solution: hook based on the actor’s cell; globally overriding `TES` fields is possible only on the main thread or during the render pass.
- **View from inside to outside:** `interiorCell` disables the sky (`AE#26237+0xB6`, mode `3`), water (`AE#36560+0x55`, called only by `Main::Draw+0x172`), and probably sunlight (verified for sky and water). For T21 and the second portal, it will have to be overridden during the pass.
- **Live street (T21):** `SetCurrentCell` disables the street in five places: `+0x21` (collisions), `+0x46D` (shader interior flag), `+0x50E` (`AE#19130` grid), `+0x53B` (LOD), `+0x54C` (slots 8/9 `SSN`). Only `State.interior` is written by `AE#105631`; three functions read it (verified).
- **An NPC at high goes through a door in two steps** (from code and in-game, control run 0.8.27 without the portal): `ActivateDoor` → `AE#37878` → animation → `Activate` starts the fade (`AE#17922+0x4EB` → `AE#39454`, verified) → after 0.5 s `AE#39457+0x1FB` activates again (verified) → `MoveRefToNewSpace` (`AE#17922+0x63A`, verified). Not at high: immediate, without fading. Everything runs on worker threads.
- **NPC transition events** are generated by `MoveRefToNewSpace` (`AE#19799`): `OnLocationChange` at `+0x8A2`, `OnAttached/DetachedFromCell` at `+0x986` (verified). `OnLocationChange` is also sent by `AE#37313+0x1D7` and two locations in `TESWorldSpace`: for `AE#37313` within one exterior worldspace, the event arrives twice (verified, batch 3). `CLOC` and `BGSActorCellEvent` are player-only.
- **The NPC path is built by `BSPathBuilder`** (`AE#91713` → `AE#91717` → door nodes `AE#91750`; verified), not `TeleportDoorSearch` (that is a coarse route for the player, maps, and quests). The segment beyond the door starts from the linked door; the marker is not used, so seamless relocation to another point in the cell probably does not break the path. Both sides must be loaded.
- **Seamless NPC passage** = portal transform + vanilla door path (`SetPosition`, rotation, `MoveRefToNewSpace`) on the main thread, without animation, fade, or relocation to the marker; `AE#37313` (`MoveTo` path) produces a duplicate `OnLocationChange` within one world. After the jump, the path follower does not replan and may send `ActivateDoor` again (`AE#90711` → `AE#90770`, verified) — suppress it for this door; a door-plane intersection detector in the tick is also the mechanism for passage of arbitrary objects (5.14, item 2).
- **Frame:** `RenderPlayerView` = `AE#36559` (`Main::Draw` in the database; `AE#36661` is not it, but rather reference collection around the player). Our pass is at `+0x17E` (`JobList::Finish` for scene lists), world-pass registration `AE#107133` at `+0x2EC`, and `RenderWorld` at `+0x841` (verified). The current submission camera is `AE#414580`; it is written by `SubmitAccumulator+0x49` (verified) and is not restored.
- **Other game views** (water, decals, local map, 3D menu, first person): each has its own accumulator and camera; shadows are not counted, TAA jitter is disabled, and they are not nested inside one another. Culling happens in static processes `AE#414842`/`AE#414845`: nested passes are impossible, and views are rendered sequentially in the `+0x17E…+0x2EC` window; for recursion, use the “N−1” scheme (previous-frame textures).
- **Compatibility with mirrors (5.14, item 5):** our `WriteCall` (`HooksCommon.h`) is installed only over the original call (verified from code): if a mirror mod hooks `+0x17E` first, the portal will not be installed. We need to chain through another mod’s thunk. Our color slot 17 matches `kLOCAL_MAP_SWAP` (`AE#16335+0x32C`).
- **T17 candidates** (**hypothesis**): the haze may be the street shadow mask on interior actors (`AE#107297+0x9B6…+0xA65`), volumetric lighting (`AE#107023`), or quad SSR; smoke may be the particle-system boundary when clipped by the portal frustum.

## Other observations

- Main thread: 27868. The SKSE `kDataLoaded` message arrives from another thread (9020).
- After every door transition or `coc`, `kSaveGame` occurs (autosave).
- `SetCenter` callers: while walking, `AE#13288+0x816`; during transitions, `AE#13428+0x235`. `DetachAll` during a world change is called from `AE#13315+0x22e`, which is near `SetCurrentCell` (`AE#13316`).
- Game strings (cell names, “Wastes”) are already in **UTF-8**. The `�` characters in the 0.2.0 parsing are an artifact of console output in cp1251, not the data.
- **Input events do not arrive on the main thread** (thread 11372 in 0.3.0).
- **Exterior cell objects (`TESObjectCELL`) are freed when their world is unloaded.**
  - How this was discovered: in 0.4.0, the plugin saved a pointer to a WhiterunWorld cell at `ActorCell leave`. After `coc Riverwood` (Tamriel), accessing it crashed: the virtual-function table had been overwritten (`rcx = 0x3F80000000000000`, call to slot `0x32`).
  - Implication for “keeping it in memory”: retaining an exterior cell across worlds is prevented by the game deleting the old world’s cell forms themselves.
  - Interiors are persistent forms; this does not apply to them.
- **F11 hang in 0.3.0.** The snapshot was deferred via `SKSE::GetTaskInterface()->AddTask`. Inside the task, the code checked the thread again and re-enqueued the task, while SKSE keeps executing the queue until it is empty. This implies that SKSE tasks run **not on the `RE::Main::threadID` thread**. Since 0.4.0, deferred work runs in the `PlayerCharacter::Update` tick without being re-enqueued.
- In the snapshot immediately after a diagonal `SetCenter`, 9 of the 25 grid cells are `null`: new cells are inserted into the grid later, together with `CellFullyLoaded`.
