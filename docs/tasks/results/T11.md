# T11. NPCs jump during transitions — result

Branch: exp/T11-npc-continuity · Build version: 0.8.27 (`dist/CellLoadResearch-0.8.27-T11-npc-continuity.zip`) · Status: 0.8.27 run confirmed (`docs/FINDINGS.md`, “Run 0.8.27”), ready to merge

## Findings
**Main point.** Both kinds of jumps are caused by the game changing the process level, not by the player changing cells. The portal makes them visible: during a transition, interior NPCs drop to middleHigh and then rise again. The mechanism is visible in game code and matches the timing of all 9 exits and 6 entries in 0.8.25–0.8.26. What drops the NPCs has not yet been verified from the trace: 0.8.27 will show this.
1. **`AE#37501` selects the level, and `AE#37503` calculates the required level** (from game code). If `AE#37503(actor)` ≠ `process+0x137`, Actor vfunc `0xF1` MoveToHigh, `0xF4` MoveToMiddleHigh, `0xF3`, or `0xF2` is called.
   - `AE#37503` returns 0–3 and has its own “keep current level” branch at `+0x143`. Paths to `AE#37501` include cell detachment (`AE#19046` → `AE#41292` → `AE#41468`: the game’s `AE#13317` and our `DetachCell`) and per-frame `AE#41294`.
2. **The “catch-up” on entry is `AE#37618`, relocation to the package point** (from game code).
   - It is called by MoveToHigh `AE#37621+0x348` (return `+0x34d`, as in the trace stack), only if the actor is not already high.
   - The condition is one of: pathing (`IMovementQueryPathingState` through `AE#41628`), `ExtraBadPosition`, null position, or previously middleLow/low.
   - The target is the package location adjusted by the navmesh (`Pathing`, `AE#401037`). Jump length is the distance to this location; time does not affect it.
3. **Steps in middleHigh are virtual path traversal** (from game code).
   - `MovementAgentPathFollowerVirtual` (`AE#92556`) sets a warp for every path point traversed: `IMovementMessageInterface::vfunc[1]` = `AE#41761`, queue `MovementControllerNPC+0x170`.
   - The queue is processed by `AE#41717+0x2af` → `AE#37877` → `AE#37313` → `SetPosition`; a door is handled by an `ActivateDoor` message (`AE#37878`). A step is the distance to a path point: targets repeat, `(530,-272)` → `(354,-213)` in 0.8.25 and 0.8.26.
4. **Timing** (from traces), relative to loading-screen closure: warps after exit +0.19…+0.22 s (7 of 8 exits, including without F8), relocation on entry +0.07…+0.08 s, autosave +0.27…+0.6 s, `promote` +1.5 s — there are no jumps after it because the path has already been traversed.
5. **`kSettleUs` is needed for attachment, not for the NPC level.** It protects against missing 3D after loading a save and against autosave. Raising NPCs when the screen closes is insufficient: during the loading screen (~0.3 s) they are already middleHigh, and MoveToHigh will relocate those who are pathing. Therefore, the implementation is “do not drop”.
6. **A control** exists only in 0.8.25 before 78.57 s (F8 disabled): exit — Saadia 186 units through `AE#41717+0x2af`, street guards — through `AE#37618`. The full control is in the 0.8.27 run.

## How to verify in the game
1. Vortex → Replace. Save near the Bannered Mare, F8 enabled. Inside, wait until Saadia walks from the kitchen to the counter. Exit, watch the portal for 10 s, then enter. Repeat 3 times.
2. Bard: request a song with spoken lines, exit in the middle of a verse, listen by the door for 15 s, then enter. Do the same with an instrumental melody.
3. Control: F8 disabled, repeat items 1–2 twice each.
4. F9 (quick load) outside, then move 2–3 cells away from the tavern and return. The game does not crash, and `status.hold.cells` is empty at a distance.
5. Expected with F8: `actor.keptHigh` when the screen opens; tavern NPCs have no `actor.level` 0→1, `actor.warpQueued`, or `actor.jump` >150 during the first 3 s after transitions (except Idolaf from the street); `actor.snap` is skipped or absent; after exit, `scene.line` has `high=true` and the song and melody continue.
6. Expected without F8 (game behavior): on exit, `actor.level` 0→1 with a stack and `actor.warpQueued` at +0.2 s; on entry, `actor.snap` is executed; a batch of `scene.line` records with `high=false`, then `scene.stop` from `AE#24126`.

## Alignment with 5.14
- **Multiple portals, any cell pairs (items 1–2).** Hooks consult only the FormID registry and do not distinguish interiors from exteriors. Each portal holds its cells with its own bit.
- **Street (T21).** It can be held only together with its physics; otherwise NPCs will freeze.
- **Any camera (item 4).** Holding does not depend on the camera. However, `AE#42210` (`MovementPlannerAgentWarp`) determines visibility using the world camera `AE#36609`.
- **Game data (item 7)** is unchanged; hooks only.

## Impact on T10
The bard remains high continuously from exit, including during the loading screen. Therefore, `AE#24236` finds `AIProcess::high`, and scene lines do not skip ahead. I expect this to eliminate the cause in T10; verification is item 2 and `scene.line`. T10 step 2 (line pause) remains as a safeguard and must use `ActorHold::HoldsCell`.
