# T30. Location-change reaction tags: what to suppress on portal entry — result

Branch: exp/T30-location-tags · Build version: none (no code) · Status: research

## Findings
Details — `docs/LOCATION_TRIGGERS.md`, sections 4.5–4.9 and 12, tag columns in sections 9 and 10.

1. **Nine tags:** `story`, `once`, `scene`, `greets`, `encounter`, `technical`, `restarts`, `placeKind`, `placeSpecific`.
   - Each has a criterion based on quest data, SM nodes, scenes, aliases, and dialogue topics (4.5).
   - Manual and data-based marking disagree on `story` (46 / 15), `greets` (8 / 2), `technical` (0 / 10), `encounter` (3 / 0) and `once` (2 / 0) (section 12). This does not affect the policy.
2. **All criteria are read from memory at `kDataLoaded` without FormIDs** (4.6, from NG headers, not verified in-game).
   - Sources: `TESQuest::data`, `objectives`, stages → `logEntry`, `scenes`, `aliases`, `storyManagerConditions`, node conditions, walking `TESTopic` (`ownerQuest`, `numTopicInfos`).
   - The quest's EditorID is stored (`TESQuest::formEditorID`), so INI keys by EditorID work for quests from other mods too.
   - Script properties via VM at `kDataLoaded` — not verified. Calls inside scripts (`StopAllSongs`) exist only in bytecode; we do not read them.
3. **`restarts` is derived from data:** `technical ∧ placeKind ∧ ¬placeSpecific ∧ ¬once ∧ ¬story ∧ ¬encounter`, i.e. "a service switch of the place kind". In vanilla this is only `BardSongStarter`, same as manually.
   - A running quest is not restarted by the Story Manager. Only a quest acting on a permanent controller can restart: `BardSongStarter` → `BardSongs`.
4. **T30 policy:** suppress ⇔ portal tag ∧ `restarts & !story & !greets & !once` ∧ "already running".
   - Across 186 quests it suppresses only `BardSongStarter`. `WIGreeting`, `WITavern`, `WIChangeLocation*`, `WICourier`, `FreeformWinterholdCollegeA` and one-off vignettes remain.
   - Of the 17 quests R8 suppressed, T30 keeps 16.
5. **The "already running" condition is mandatory.** In a world without loading screens every entry goes through a portal, and only `BardSongStarter` starts the song. Without this condition a bard who was silent would never sing.
   - Check: the controller (a script property, `BardSongs`) has a scene playing with an actor in the destination cell (`BGSScene::isPlaying`). Not verified.
6. **R8 is expressed with the same tags:** `!story & !once & !placeSpecific & (placeKind | encounter)`. Matches 4.2 fully on the table. The first-iteration T16 cache (tag mask + expression) will accept the T30 policy without rework.
7. **The vanilla merchant greeting is the `CLOC` quest `WIGreeting`** (4.9).
   - Fires on `L2∋LocTypeDwelling`; all 30 shops carry this tag.
   - Alias `Merchant`: `JobMerchantFaction`, `LocTypeStore`, `GetOffersServicesNow`, `GetDetected(player)`. Scene `WIGreetingScene` with `BeginOnQuestStart`, no `RunOnce`.
   - Second layer — `Hello` lines (6 687 `INFO` in `Dialogue<city>`, for merchants `GetIsID` + `GetOffersServicesNow`). They are started by the player approaching, not by `CLOC`; T16 does not touch them.
   - Tavern: `WITavern`, alias `Innkeeper`, scene `WITavernGreeting`.
8. **5.14.** The policy looks at quest tags, not at the kind of cells. It is the same on entry and exit, for any cell pairs and for many portals: the tag is tied to its own portal.

## How to verify in-game (for T16, second iteration)
1. Log at `kDataLoaded`: tag mask per `CLOC` quest; `restarts` and the "suppress" decision — only for `BardSongStarter` (with USSEP too).
2. Tavern, the bard is silent (first entry), enter through the portal: `story.start BardSongStarter`, the song starts.
3. A song is playing, exit and enter through the portal: `story.skip BardSongStarter`, the song does not break.
4. A shop during opening hours, enter through the portal: `story.start WIGreeting`, the merchant greets. Tavern: `story.start WITavern`.
5. Exit outside through the portal: `WIChangeLocation*` and `WICourier` — `story.start`, if the chance rolled.

## Open questions
The user decides; recommendations are mine.
- **Radiant encounters** (`DLC1EclipseAttack*`, `WIChangeLocation08/09`, disguised vampire) on portal entry. Recommendation: keep, as in vanilla; the "T30 + encounters" variant is described in 4.8.
- **Repeatable greetings** (`WIGreeting`, `WITavern`, `WIChangeLocation01/02`). Recommendation: keep, as the user asked. If they become annoying — a separate rate-limiting tag, not suppression.
- **How to determine "already running".** Recommendation: a script property via VM on the quest's first run; fallback — `sRestartsController=BardSongStarter:BardSongs`. Needs in-game verification.
- **Journal-less dungeon quests** are not `story` per data (46). Not important now. It will matter if the policy starts suppressing anything other than `restarts`: then add to `story` the sign "`placeSpecific` + scenes or stages".
