## What was found
- **A remote player is always a `Character`-NPC**, there is only one real player per client: TE creates a `TESNPC` from the appearance (`Actor.cpp:208`), SkyMP clones the NPC `0x10D13E` with a "do nothing" package. No one creates a second `PlayerCharacter`: confirms T31.
- **Nobody retains cells beyond the vanilla grid.** TE polls `TES::currentGridX/centerGridX`, the 5×5 grid is hard-coded (`GridCellCoords.h:10`), no copy of a remote player is created outside the grid; an actor without a player in the area is dropped from sync. SkyMP does not load cells (the server reads `.esp`, grid 4096 for "world or cell").
- **Doors:** TE — nothing special (each client does the vanilla loading). SkyMP — the server decides the teleport (`MpObjectReference.cpp:1466`), the client hides a player from another cell.
- **AI and the uniqueness of the player:** TE patches three places (`AE#42704` detection, `IsFleeing`, the player faction `0xDB1`); combat and AI packages are disabled code. SkyMP removes the vanilla world entirely.
- **TE hooks** (80 of them) — AE-IDs. Overlap with ours: `AE#19790` `SetPosition`, `AE#56626` `MoveTo_Impl` (a call inside it); nearby: `AE#37356` right below our `AE#37350`, `ActivateRef` `AE#19796` above our door. The rest is new.
- **Unsolved everywhere (threatens us):** sound through the door, NPCs outside the field of view (SkyMP ROADMAP), invisibility after a cell change ([#610](https://github.com/tiltedphoques/TiltedEvolution/issues/610)), the `Unequip` loop after an ownership change ([#810](https://github.com/tiltedphoques/TiltedEvolution/issues/810)).
- **Not reached:** sources of the old SkyMP (gitlab), the closed Skyrim Together v1, "Keizaal Online" (Keizaal is a Wabbajack modlist without code). The match of SkyOn with "Skyrim Online" is unconfirmed.
