# T12. Custom color correction for the portal image — result

Branch: exp/T12-portal-color · Build version: 0.8.29 (`dist/CellLoadResearch-0.8.29-T12-portal-color.zip`) · Status: 0.8.29 run successful (`logs/run-0.8.29`), distant fog fixed afterward, ready to merge

## 0.8.28 hang on F11 and 0.8.29 fix (coordinator, from code and logs)
- **Cause** (from game code, `d3d11.dll`, and the 0.8.28 DLL). `FindAdaptTargets` iterated `renderTargets` using `std::size` = 125 (`kVRTOTAL` in the FLAT build), while 1.6.1170 actually has 116 targets. `depthStencils` follows: candidate 122 = `depthStencils[1].depthSRV`.
  - Its “`GetDesc`” (slot 10) in `d3d11.dll` is a trampoline `sub rcx,0A0h; jmp QueryInterface` into an adjacent vtable. It calls `QueryInterface` on `[this−0x50]` with `&desc` instead of an IID and writes `nullptr` through a garbage `r8`. In `TraceAdaptTargets`, `r8` remains from freeing strings in the previous iteration.
  - On frame 0 this passed silently (122 became a candidate: `desc` remained zero). F11 invoked it twice more. The last record is `ldr` (`fflush`); `portal.adapt` (also `fflush`) was not written. There is no `hang.txt`: the watchdog monitored only an active portal. In the WARP test, the same call crashes (0xC0000005).
- **Fix.** `RenderTargetCount` (114/116 through the NG versioned offset), `TargetTexture` through `QueryInterface(ID3D11Texture2D)`. Verified against the 0.8.29 machine code.
- **Protection.** `[Portal] bDumpLdr`, `bDumpAdaptTargets`, `bDumpGraded` (default 1). The watchdog also monitors for 10 s after F11. `Trace::Write` replaces `nan`/`inf` outside strings with `null` (previously occurred in `worldUnnamed58`).

## Findings (research, 0.8.25 screenshots)
Scripts: `<scratchpad>\T12\`. Game shaders disassembled from `Skyrim - Shaders.bsa` (`D3DDisassemble`); № is the DXBC block number in the archive.
- **The quad distorts the image before the exterior ImageSpace** (from screenshots). Portal→main aligned by homography, correlation 0.994: `kMAIN = 0.62·x + (0.008, 0.012, 0.013)`, where predictions ≥ 1.05 yield 0.994–0.999.
  - The quad shader (№ 746, 2395) writes `lerp(c, fog, f) × FogNearColor.w` without `saturate`. A quad without `NiAlphaProperty` enters the opaque phase, and the exterior ISSAOComposite (№ 15990–15994) performs AO, SSR, fog, `× FogNearColor.w`, and `mad_sat` (from game code). This is the source of the HDR fire clipping found by T13.
  - `FogNearColor.w` = `State::invFrameBufferRange` (+0x9C), statically 0.8333 (`AE#23079` — console `sfbr`).
  - Effect fog is set by `AE#107524 +0xE7…+0x181` from `AE#105637` (= `ShadowSceneNode[i] + 0x220`).
- **The pass lacks the interior composite** (from screenshots). The interior column is ×0.75 from the portal, and the far door ×1.88 (haze). The portal texture has no `×0.833`, interior fog, or SAO; effects receive exterior fog.
- **ISHDR formula** (№ 15836) and parameters (`AE#107185`, ImageSpaceEffectHDR vfunc 7):
  - k = avg.y/avg.x; T = Le(1+p·Le)/(1+Le), p = 1/(fReinhardWhiteScale·white)²;
  - saturation, tint, avg.x + contrast·(brightness·c − avg.x), `saturate`.
  - Simulation: the same texture with exterior parameters is 36% brighter and loses 44% of its color. This is the main visible difference.
- **Adaptation** (from game code): 1×1 target on t2, .x slow, .y fast (№ 15848, 15852). At rest, k = 1. Targets alternate according to flag `AE#415054` (`AE#107183 +0x296`).
- **Recommendation a)** accepted by the coordinator: b) erases translucency in front of the door, remains without TAA, and does not work in mirrors and nested portals (5.14, items 4–5).

## What changed
- **`src/PortalGrade.cpp` (new).** Shaders are compiled by `D3DCompile` from `d3dcompiler_47.dll` (`LoadLibrary`). Dedicated `scratch` and `graded` targets; D3D state is saved and fully restored (`ContextState`).
  - **Layer 1 `Composite`** — on the first effect technique in the pass (where the game performs compositing), or after the pass if there are no effects: `saturate(ifr · lerp(c, interior fog(depth), f))`.
  - **Layer 2 `Grade`** — after `RestoreLook`, into `graded`: `x' = A⁻¹(T_exterior⁻¹(T_interior(c)))`.
    - The interior look comes from `ExtraCellImageSpace`, otherwise from a capture inside, otherwise neutral. The exterior look comes from `ImageSpaceManager` data plus `fReinhardWhiteScale`/`fGlobal*Boost` by name.
    - Exterior avg/k comes from the 1×1 target (iterate targets ≤ 2×2, `base + 2·flag`); fallback — k = 1 and avg = 0.25.
    - Interior avg is read from inside without stopping the GPU, once every 30 frames.
    - A is the quad fog and exterior composite, `[Portal] iQuadFogModel` = 0/1/2.
    - With ENB or `bUseFilmicCurve`, layer 2 is disabled and the reason is written to the trace.
- **`PortalImage.cpp`.** Full-frame ImageSpace substitution removed. F2 = `TogglePortalGrading` (`[Hotkeys] iPortalGrading`), cycling 2 → 0 → 1. During the pass, `UseInteriorLook`/`RestoreLook` substitutes interior fog: captured inside or from XCLL with a template. `FogJson`.
- **`PortalRender.cpp`.** Layer calls. F11 also writes `graded-N.dds`. Names of group 5 shapes are recorded before `DrainPasses` (SEH).
- **Hook** `ImageSpaceEffectHDR::Render` (vfunc 1) in `Hooks.cpp`: F11 writes `ldr-N.dds` and `portal.adapt` (values of 1×1 targets).
- **`Portal.cpp`.** `SwapQuadTexture` displays `graded` at stage 2.
- **Trace:** `portal.grade` (init, start, toggle, F11: both looks, fog, avg, A), `portal.adaptTargets`. Portal status fields `grade`, `fog`, `refractionPasses`, `refractionShapes`.
- **T13 diagnostics (trace only):**
  - `passEnum`, `accumulationHint`, `shaderFlags` for observed shapes, plus up to 8 lit shapes with `kRefraction` (heat haze);
  - names in group 5;
  - `fog` in portal status and in the interior probe;
  - particle RGB in `FireSampleJson`;
  - `billboards`: fire `NiBillboardNode` mode and angles of its axes to the eye.
- **Dead code removed per T13.**
  - `iControllerUpdate`: behavior 1 retained.
  - `iParticleFacing`: behavior 2 retained.
  - `bLightEffects` and lamp sleeping in `BeforeEffectPasses`. **Behavior changes:** the INI had 0; now effects with `kEffectLighting` receive copies of interior lamps, as in the game.
- **INI:** new `iGradeStages=2`, `iQuadFogModel=2`, `iPortalGrading=60`; removed keys deleted. `docs/FEATURES.md` (INI list), `docs/PORTABILITY.md` (flag `AE#415054`, adaptation-target layout, raw `BSFogProperty` fields).
- **`tools/portal_dump_to_png.py`:** formats 24 (R10G10B10A2) and 28/29 (R8G8B8A8) for `ldr-N`.
- **Build:** no errors or warnings from `src/`. HLSL separately verified with `d3dcompiler_47`: 3 entry points, no warnings. Not launched in the game.

## 0.8.29 run (in game, 13 F11 screenshots, no hangs)
- Stage 2: fire and light almost as from inside, slightly darker inside. No brightness oscillation, fringing, or overexposure. The same at an angle. Stage 0: fire larger than in 0.8.26, smoke and brightness unchanged.
- `portal.adaptTargets`: 116 targets, candidates 31–34. Pair 31/33 (2×2, R16G16B16A16) alternates, 32 and 34 (1×1) are empty; x = y, that is, k = 1.
- **Interior hazy from a distance** (screenshots 5 and 8: 1341 and 1035 units to the door, ≈ 19 and 15 m; `portal-4`, `portal-7` already after layer 1). Interior fog (128–2000 units) was calculated from depth relative to the pass eye, but the eye is outside: 50–80% fog.
  - **Fixed in 0.8.30:** interior fog starts at the door plane, `max(z − g_eyeDistance, 0)` in the composite, and the effect-fog near/far shift by `g_eyeDistance`. The pass camera looks along the door normal, so this is the exact path inside. The exterior fogs its own part on the quad.

## 0.8.30 run (in game, a — `iQuadFogModel=2`, b — `1`)
- Distant haze became weaker, but the distant portal is brighter and “blown out” in both variants. With 2, the portal is brighter than from inside; with 1, brightness matches the interior.
- Measurement (`main = α·graded + β` at the door location, 992–2280 units): α ≈ 0.88 × model 1, model 2 twice as bright. **Since 0.8.31, the default is `iQuadFogModel=1`.** The adaptation-support hypothesis was not confirmed: with model 1, brightness nearby matches the interior.
- The remainder is a gray additive term β that increases with distance (0.08 → 0.32), 2–3 times greater than exterior fog. This and smoke disappearing without the fire in frame are task T17 (`docs/tasks/T17-portal-picture.md`).
