## What was found
- **There is one exterior Havok world per game, not per world** — `GetbhkWorld` (`AE#18995+0xF`) for any exterior cell reads `*AE#400440`; only `AE#19193+0x111` (creation, from the `GridCellArray` constructor `AE#15952`) and `AE#19194+0xB2` write it. At gates, bodies from two worlds will end up in the same `hkpWorld`.
- **"Current location" helpers do not see the attached interior:** `TES::Pick` (`AE#13371+0xC/+0x27`, actor and player LOS, 11 callers), `TES::GetCell(pos)` (`AE#13322`, 8), `AE#13306` (5), `Pathing` vtable 2–6 (`AE#30633+0x10`: `interiorCell`, otherwise `exteriorBuffer` `+0x67`). I2 needs a hook based on the actor's cell; global substitution is unsuitable: `Pick` is also called from jobs (`AE#42744` ← `AE#39946` ← `ProcessActorAnimSG`).
- **`TES::interiorCell` is read by 92 functions, `worldSpace` by 47 directly + 47 through `TES::GetWorldSpace` (`AE#13314`).** There are 6 writers of `interiorCell` and 8 of `worldSpace`, including `MapMenu::AE_53094+0x147` (clears `worldSpace`) — a precedent for substitution in the game itself.
- **Rendering:** only `AE#105631` writes `State.interior` (`+0x44`), and 3 functions read it (`AE#107229+0x204`, `AE#106516+0x4EF`, `AE#106945+0x272`); the shader grid center `+0xA8…+0xB8` — `AE#13288`, `AE#13317`, `AE#13287`; `SetPortalGraph` (`AE#106385`) removes all light internally (`+0x50D` `RemoveAllLights`). Reads — T44.
- **Entering an interior disables the street in five places** in `SetCurrentCell`: `+0x21` (`toggleCollision=0`), `+0x46D` (`interior=1`), `+0x50E` (`AE#19130(gridCell,0)`), `+0x53B` (hide LOD), `+0x54C` (slots 8/9 `SSN`) — a map for T21.
- **The sky cell is updated only for the current world** (`Main::Update+0x7A5` `GetSkyCell` → `+0x7B2` `AE#19012`); persistent references do not live in the world beyond the gates (what happens to city NPCs there is an assumption).
- **Zone level** (`BGSEncounterZone::AE_18199`) is set only in `SetCurrentCell+0x288` and `AE#13315+0x215`; an interior attached bypassing them does not receive a zone level (assumption: there are no other paths).
- **Acoustics:** the listener flag `bhkWorld+0xC5C8`→`+0x18` is set by `SetParentCell+0x258` only for the player's world and cleared by `AE#19130+0x1BE` — relevant to T23 (purpose is an assumption).
- `AE#13308` (61 callers) checks only `cellState` — it is not a singleton and is valid for an attached interior.

## Key points for T29, T21, and 5.14
- **T29:** the portal has held cells, its own portal graph, a render-state snapshot for the pass, and a zone-initialization flag. New hooks (in): `AE#13371`, `AE#13322`, `AE#13306`, `Pathing` 2–6 — based on the actor's cell. Substitute `TES` fields only on M or within a render frame, not on jobs.
- **T21:** a living street from inside — skip block `SetCurrentCell+0x49C…+0x54C` and `+0x21` in the existing `AE#13316` detour; rendering the street from the interior — substitution for the duration of the pass. A second portal on an interior door — the "interior ↔ interior" scenario (simplest: separate Havok worlds).
- **Gates (5.14, item 2):** `TES` singletons (grid, `worldSpace`, `ExteriorCellLoader` key without a world, Havok world) are the limit; keep world B in its own cell list bypassing `TES`. A staged path: "window" without physics → sky cell and helpers (`AE#13321`, `AE#13319`) → second `bhkWorld` + hooking `AE#18995` and 23 functions.
