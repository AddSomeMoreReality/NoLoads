# T31. Virtual player behind the portal — result

Branch: exp/T31-virtual-player · Build version: none (no code) · Status: research

## Findings
Tables and evidence — `docs/VIRTUAL_PLAYER.md`. Everything is from the 1.6.1170 code unless stated "in-game".

1. **There is a single player identity:** pointer `AE#403521`, handle `AE#403520` (stored nearby, `0x1431874F4`), and FormID `0x14`. There is no function; `Game.GetPlayer` (`AE#55469`) is `mov rax,[AE#403521]; ret`.
   - Pointer: 2 843 references in 1 349 functions (own scan, matched), 9 instruction forms: 2 604 `mov`, 223 `cmp`, 13 `cmov`, 1 `add`, 2 writes (`AE#36629` — creation).
   - Handle: 112 references in 102 functions. `formID == 0x14`: 2 places. No checks by the `PlayerCharacter` vtable.
   - Loaded pointer: as an argument — 1 761 places, comparison — 424, `PlayerCharacter` fields (≥ `0x2B8`) — 323, field writes — 113, vcall — 247 (`Get3D1` 50). Only 2 places cache the pointer into objects: the player is stored by handle.
   - Position: 57 places, 153 reads of X/Y/Z ("149" in `DISTANCE_CHECKS.md` is the fields). **Position-only** places (position, angle, cell, and nothing else): 90 places in 78 functions, among them `AE#18889`, `AE#36661`, `AE#39322`, `AE#37618`, `AE#39673`.
2. **Frequency (F3 0.8.24, in-game):** the player position is read 699 times per frame outside, about 94 % of them outside the "position-only" places, mostly via `GetDistance` from `AE#39637` (already through the door). F3 does not see the pointer load; its frequency is unmeasured.
3. **The far side has no thread of its own** (traces 0.8.32 and 0.8.27, in-game):
   - the interior Havok step runs on one of the six `BSJobs` worker threads, a different one each time (`stepThread`);
   - the same threads run doors, `CellFullyLoaded`, Papyrus, and scenes, and per F3 stacks — actor updates (`AE#39089`, `AE#41371`, `AE#42742`, `AE#37350`) and the cell walk (`AE#18871`) under `AE#69380` ← `BSJobs::JobThread`;
   - the interior NPC level is also changed by the loading thread (`AE#37501` ← `AE#13104`).
   - `ARCHITECTURE.md`, 3.5: the "physics thread" is a job on the shared pool.
4. **Options** (table — `VIRTUAL_PLAYER.md`, section 4):
   - A, a second `PlayerCharacter` — infeasible. The engine does not see it (pointer, handle, FormID), and 12 overridden vfuncs read the global pointer themselves.
   - B, an `Actor` double — infeasible as a substitution target. 323 places read fields beyond `Character`, 424 comparisons and the handle do not recognize it. Without substitution, the engine never asks for it.
   - C, time-based substitution — partial. The actor update is split into 6+ job kinds: they would have to be taken out of the game's lists. Readers in other threads during the phase are unverified.
   - D, by places — the foundation, works.
   - E, substitution of the pointer per thread — the mechanics exist (rewrite the 2 843 places as stubs, about 20–40 cycles, an estimate), but there is no object to substitute.
5. **The user's hypothesis (E) is wrong in two places:**
   - there is no dedicated thread;
   - there is nothing to substitute the pointer with: there is no second player, and a shadow copy breaks 424 comparisons, the handle, and 113 writes.
   - Hardware breakpoints do not fit either: every worker thread would have to be armed, and the exception would be paid on every read. F3 handled about 3 200 hits per frame, but that is too expensive for permanent operation.
   - **The way out:** `thread_local` works per frame, without a thread of its own (like `Portal::ActorScope`). What must be substituted is not the object but the **place**: the 90 "position-only" places in the far side's frame. And a census of all 2 843 places with counters.
6. **Verdict:** do not build a full virtual player. D remains the foundation, E′ (E + C, places only) — the prototype. It partially covers T22 (the tool and direct position reads). It does not cover T21, T23, T27 and the `PortalActors` hooks (camera, `kNotVisible`, LOD, fading, `GetDistance`): those depend on the camera or on the player as an argument. **T22 need not wait.**

## Prototype brief: T32 "Census of player reads and player position in the far side's frame"
- Branch `exp/T32-player-reads`, module `PlayerReads.cpp` (hooks per rule 3.6), INI `[PlayerReads]`, disabled by default.
- **Step 1, census (behavior unchanged).**
  - At plugin load, find all RIP references in `.text` to the address `RELOCATION_ID(517014, 403521)`: 9 forms by bytes.
  - Replace each place (verify the bytes, skip a foreign `E8`/`E9`) with a `jmp` to a stub in the trampoline. The stub counts the read as "in frame / out of frame" (`pushfq`/`popfq`) and executes the original instruction.
  - Generalize `Portal::ActorScope` into the far side's frame and also place it around our own interior walk (`AE#13291`/`AE#13325`) and the interior Havok step.
  - Dump on a hotkey: places with `AE#id+off`, in-frame and out-of-frame counts.
- **Step 1 in-game verification:** portal at the Bannered Mare, stand at the door for 30 s, dump.
  - Criterion: no crashes, frame time ±0.2 ms of control.
  - The dump contains in-frame places, `AE#18889` expected among them.
  - The number of missed places is logged.
- **Step 2 (after analyzing the dump):** "position-only" places (verified by the Zydis decoder at startup) read a block in-frame of the form: position and angle through the door, cell beyond the door. `GetDistance` through the door takes the position from the same block.
  - Criterion: with `bDistanceThroughDoor=1` NPC behavior is no worse than 0.8.32; in the trace, the interior's `AE#18889` sees the player at the door.
  - With step 2 disabled — match control. A run with Engine Fixes — at the end.
