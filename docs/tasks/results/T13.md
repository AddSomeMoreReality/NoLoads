# T13. Fire is smaller and there is no smoke through the portal — result

Branch: exp/T13-fire-smoke · Build version: none (research only) · Status: research

## What was found
- **The simulation is the same, there are no fewer particles and they are not smaller** (per the trace). The tavern hearth is `Campfire01Burning`, systems `FlamesSmall03`, `Firearticles`, `smoke02`. From inside (`probe.fires`, 33 samples) and through the portal (`portal.fires`, 7 statuses) in 0.8.25: particles 7 / 8 / 30 vs 7 / 8 / 30; median `size` 0.70 / 1.38 / 1.35 vs 0.72 / 1.38 / 1.21; `radius` 15.9 / 19.8 / 9.8 vs 16.1 / 20.0 / 9.8; `alpha`, `age`, `lifespan` match. `emitScale` = `sizeScale` = 1 in both cases, so `BSPSysLODModifier` (`AE#108480`) does not cut. Materials and `shaderFlags` are the same for all shapes of the hearth.
- **No parts of systems are lost during culling** (per the trace). For all six effects of the hearth, `noPasses` = 0, `passCalls` ≈ the number of passes (`smoke02`: 2320 of 2647 in 0.8.25, 2106 of 2284 in 0.8.26; fewer where the hearth is off-screen). `leftoverGroups` = 0x00000, `fault` empty. The `hiddenEffects` fields no longer exist in the code.
- **Smoke is drawn into the portal texture** (per the trace, F11 screenshots). In the alpha channel of `portal-2.dds` (0.8.25) and `portal-1.dds` (0.8.26), a smoke column is visible above the hearth: about 7 000 pixels with alpha < 0.9, around 1.0 elsewhere. In these pixels RGB is (0.151, 0.140, 0.117), nearby (0.181, 0.141, 0.097): the smoke is there, but dark and barely noticeable. Above the flame alpha > 1 — that is additive flame.
- **Smoke goes to the alpha group, not to group 5** (per the game code). `RegisterObject` (`AE#106570 + 0x11D`) distributes a pass by `BSRenderPass::accumulationHint` (+0x1C), jump table `0x1414B2690`: 1 → alpha list (`AE#107669`, `batchRenderer + 0xF0` or an ordered group), 2 → 3 (decals, only with `drawDecals`), 3 → 4, **4 → 5**, 5 → 6, 6 → 0, 7 → 1, 9 → 7, 11 → 9, 13 → 10, 14 → 11, 15 → 8, 16 → 12, 17/18 → 13, the rest → technique buckets. `BSEffectShaderProperty::GetRenderPasses` (`AE#106876 + 0x1BD…0x242`) gives effects only 1, 2, 3, 9, and 0x12; the translucent `smoke02` has hint 1. `SubmitAccumulator` (`AE#106436` → vfunc 0x2A/0x2B → `AE#106583`, `AE#106584`) draws groups 9, 8, 1, 0, 13, 10, 11, 12, 7 and the alpha list (`AE#106585 + 0x148`: `mov rcx,[rcx+0F0h]; call AE#107629`). Flame under the same `OrderedRenderingNode` is visible, so the path is the same.
- **Group 5 is heat shimmer, and there is none through the portal** (per game code and trace). Only `BSLightingShaderProperty::GetRenderPasses` sets hint 4: `AE#106516 + 0x55B`: `test r14,8004h` (`kRefraction` | `kTempRefraction`), `+0x61A`: `mov byte ptr [rsi+1Ch],4`. For the hearth it is `Plane05:0` (flags `0x0000803182418309`, bit 15; 174 units above the hearth, radius 194), for fireplaces `Plane03:0`. The game draws groups 6 and 5 only from `AE#36559 + 0x870`: `AE#106598` (group 5 count) → `AE#107145` → `AE#106599`, target 0xD = `kREFRACTION_NORMALS` (`+0xAA`, `+0x18C`), then the ImageSpace distortion reads it. Our `DrainPasses` discards it. This is not smoke but air shimmer above the fire.
- **The portal quad clips HDR and darkens the picture** (per trace, F11 screenshots; where exactly is a guess). `kMAIN` with the portal on screen (`main-2`, `main-3` 0.8.25, `main-1` 0.8.26) never exceeds 0.999, with a cluster of values 0.993–0.999 at the ceiling. From inside (`main-1` 0.8.25) up to 1.37. In the portal texture of the same frames the flame reaches 1.9–3.0. On `main-3` the door fills the whole screen, and over the flat floor `kMAIN` ≈ 0.67 × texture. The bright core of the flame loses HDR and bloom: hence "fire is smaller and dimmer". Probably the quad's `BSEffectShader` (without `NiAlphaProperty`) does `saturate` on output. The 0.67 scale is unexplained.
- **The portal fog is the street fog** (per plugin code, the impact is a guess). `UseInteriorLook` substitutes only the background and the sun, while the accumulator takes `activeShadowSceneNode = WorldScene()` with the street's `fogProperty` (`ShadowSceneNode + 0x220`). The whitish haze above the hearth from inside (`main-1`, before ImageSpace) may be partly the interior fog, which the pass lacks.
- **Mode 0x40/0x41** (per game code). The game draws the world with flags `0x41 | jitter` and `0x40 | jitter` (`AE#107142 + 0x111`, `+0x2D1`), the portal — with 0 (`SubmitAccumulator(…, 0)`). In `AE#106584` bit 0x40 enables only the stencil mask for the ImageSpace effect. No visible impact on the fire.
- **False leads**: budget, LOD, controllers, fading, `hiddenEffects`, group 5 for smoke. Soft particles are ruled out for flame (0.8.24); not checked separately for smoke.

## Dead code candidates (coordinator's decision)
- Remove `iControllerUpdate=2`: 0.8.13, no difference from 1. Mode 1 is needed, otherwise the flame freezes. The key can be removed entirely.
- Remove `bLightEffects` together with the lamp suppression in `BeforeEffectPasses` and keep the behavior at `1`, as in the game. All eight effects of the hearth have `lit` = false, so the key changes nothing for fire (0.8.12). With `0`, lit interior effects lose their light and diverge from the inside view. Keep the `passCalls` counter.
- Remove `iParticleFacing` 0 and 1: `packHeldPortal` = `packCalls` (903 of 903), `packOriginDrift` = 0, i.e. 0 ≡ 1. Only 2 is needed. The camera variant from the plan, item 4, removes it too.

## Code plan (diagnostic build first, trace only)
Diagnostics, files `PortalEffects.cpp`, `PortalRender.cpp`, `PortalLights.cpp`, `Probe.cpp`:
- in the `GetRenderPasses` hook (already exists) write `accumulationHint` and `passEnum` of observed shapes, add `BSLightingShaderProperty` with `kRefraction` (`Plane05:0`) to the observation;
- in `DrainPasses` before `AE#106600(…,5)` list the geometry names of `geometryGroups[5]->passList`;
- `fogProperty` of `ShadowSceneNode[0]` (near, far, power, clamp, colors `unk60…78`) — in the portal status and in the inside probe; cell fog from `GetLighting()`;
- particle RGB (`colors`) in `FireSampleJson`, currently only alpha there;
- for the fire's `NiBillboardNode`: mode and angle between its axis and the player's view.

Fixes based on the results:
1. **Portal HDR** (with T12): output the portal picture to `kMAIN` without `saturate` and without ×0.67. This is either T12's own composition (own shader) or replacing the quad's shader. First check whether raising the quad's `baseColorScale` to 2 lifts the `kMAIN` maximum above 1.
2. **Interior fog**: in `UseInteriorLook`/`RestoreLook` (`PortalImage.cpp`, `SavedLook` in `PortalState.h`) substitute the `fogProperty` fields with values sampled from inside, like `imageSpace`. No hooks needed. If the shader takes fog from frame constants, one more call that loads them is needed (find it).
3. **Shimmer (group 5)**, as a second step: the portal's own refraction-normals target and distortion at composition time (T12). Must not draw into the game's `kREFRACTION_NORMALS`: `AE#106599` clears and draws it after our pass, and the `DrainPasses` rule forbids holding passes until then.
4. **The pass camera = the player's view through the door** (5.14, item 4). Rotated like the transplanted view, frustum clipped by the door rectangle on screen, near plane at the door (oblique, Lengyel). Then particles, `NiBillboardNode`, soft particles, and LOD face the eye by themselves; detour `AE#77463` and `iParticleFacing` are not needed.
